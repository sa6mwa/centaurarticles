\noindent This essay was written through conversation and collaboration between Michel Blomgren and AI. The interview invitations of 23 and 27 October 2026 explicitly encouraged use of a preferred AI agent. Both invitations and the recruiter's follow-up of 4 November were read during preparation of this edition. The employer and interviewers remain unnamed.

The source-code totals are development snapshots recorded during preparation of this essay. CAI: 314,975 total lines, comprising 183,548 implementation and 131,427 test lines (41.73\% tests). Of these, 288,577 are C, including 109,723 C test lines. The larger stack count describes checked-out development worktrees on one VPS; it is not a deduplicated inventory of shipped production code. CAI belongs to that wider stack and is not added again. The separately developed \texttt{libailo} is excluded from the VPS count. The figures describe the development stack and its test investment.
